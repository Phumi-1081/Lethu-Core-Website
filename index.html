What "handled" looks like

Two real examples of the work, with client names left out. This is the level of follow-through you're buying.

One hardware ticket, eleven steps, zero customer chasing

A customer reports a faulty component and can't install the replacement themselves. We request an install code from the technical team against the customer's address, confirm it with the customer, issue the invoice, and give the technician the go-ahead while flagging that the part is still in transit. We then chase the delivery estimate, go back to the repair team to book the technician for after that date, confirm the date with the customer, and hold the ticket open until the delivery partner sends photo confirmation that assembly is complete. The customer sends one email. We send about fifteen.

Knowing which promotion actually won the customer back

Every win-back offer is logged against the customer's previous plan, the plan they returned on, and the date they reactivated. After one quarter you stop guessing which discount works and start seeing which offer brings back the customers who stay. That tracker takes a few minutes a day to maintain and changes what you spend on retention.
